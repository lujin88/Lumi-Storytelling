# Brief

## Goal
Help someone turn a project they did into a story they can present in an interview, a report or a portfolio, without starting from a blank page.

## Users
Individual contributors (designers, PMs, engineers) preparing to present their own work, usually under time pressure and with scattered notes.

## Constraints
- One self-contained HTML file; no build step.
- The user brings their own AI keys; keys stay in the browser.
- Bilingual UI (Chinese / English); light and dark themes follow the time of day.
